# What's New — 1.6.4

Covers everything since 1.6.3, the version on the App Store. Platform v1.6.4 changed the engineering
wiki and tooling only. The app adds the Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib (push, sheet, card and
widget), but it is switched on only in TestFlight builds: an App Store build of 1.6.4 leaves it off,
so for App Store readers this version changes nothing they can see. Rewrite this text before
submitting a build that turns the Hukamnama on. The App Store build is Gurmukhi-only.

```text
Reliability improvements, in step with gurbanisoul.com.

The text of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji is unchanged.
```
