# What's New — 1.7.2

Covers everything since 1.7.1, the version on the App Store. Platform v1.7.2 adds a monthly archive,
a share button and a methodology page to the daily Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib on
gurbanisoul.com, and resolves Vaar days whose SGPC page prints its salok and pauri labels. In the app,
the Hukamnama stays switched on only in TestFlight builds: an App Store build of 1.7.2 leaves it off,
so App Store readers see no change inside the app. The text below is the 1.7.1 text the owner
approved, unchanged.

```text
Reliability improvements, in step with gurbanisoul.com — where the daily Hukamnama from Sri Harmandir Sahib is now published.

The text of Sri Guru Granth Sahib Ji is unchanged.
```
